🥣 Golden Ladle Bistro

Run a tiny bistro through the lunch rush. Orders land on the board — cook them, plate them, serve them before patience runs out.

  1. Grab & cook. Click an ingredient on the counter, then click a station: board chops, grill grills, pot boils. Tomato + potato go in the same pot — scoop out one ladled soup. Click a chopping board (or press C) to chop faster.
  2. Plate it. Click the assembly plate to add each finished piece. A green tick on a ticket means that piece is already on the plate.
  3. Serve it. When a ticket glows green and says SERVE, hit SERVE (or Space). Fast service earns a bigger tip; three lost orders end the shift early.
